Privacy

Emberhut by Symploc · Last updated 26 November 2026

Emberhut by Symploc is made by Symploc (Symploc, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company; "we", "us"). This page explains what data the Emberhut software and the emberhut.com website handle, and where it goes. The short version: Emberhut runs on your computer and keeps your data there, and this website doesn't track you.

The Emberhut software

Emberhut is self-hosted. Its server (the "hut") runs on a computer you control and stores your conversations, tasks, notes, memories, settings and connected-account credentials on that computer. We don't operate a copy of your agent, and the software doesn't send your content to us.

  • Your devices. The desktop and phone apps connect to your own hut. They pair with a one-time code and keep their session in your device's keychain.
  • Services you connect. Emberhut does its work through services you choose and sign in to, such as the AI model provider behind its agent, your email, calendar, Slack, WhatsApp and your browser. What Emberhut sends them to do a job (for example a message you ask it to read or write) is handled by those services under their own terms and privacy policies, not ours.
  • No telemetry. The software doesn't send usage analytics or crash reports to us. If that ever changes, it will be opt-in and described here first.
  • Updates. When an app checks for updates, it downloads them from our release servers, which see an ordinary web request (your IP address and the app's version).

This website

  • emberhut.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or third-party trackers.
  • The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data needed to deliver and protect the site, such as your IP address, browser and the pages requested, under its privacy policy.
  • If you email us, for example to join the waitlist, we keep your email address and message to reply and to tell you about Emberhut. We don't sell it or share it for marketing. Ask us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

Because your Emberhut data lives on your own computer, you can export or delete it at any time by managing that computer's files. For anything we hold, such as an email you sent us, you can ask us to show, correct or delete it.

Children

Emberhut isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change how Emberhut or this site handles data, we'll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
