A warm home for your personal agent

Emberhut is a personal agent that lives on your own machine. Quiet while you're away, ready the moment you need it, on your desktop, your phone, your browser and your Mac.

The name

The coal that keeps glowing

An ember is what's left when the flames die down: a coal that keeps glowing, holding its heat, ready to catch again the moment you breathe on it.

Emberhut is a small, warm home where your agent lives. It keeps working quietly while you're away, remembers what you told it, and is there the second you come back.

Not a chat window that forgets you when you close it. A place, on a computer you own, that's always a little bit lit.

Still glowing at 3 a.m.

What it does

An agent that keeps working while you don't

Talk to it like a person, give it the boring parts, and come back to things done, or to the one question only you can answer.

Rooms, not threads

Each room is its own conversation with its own agent, so work for one thing never spills into another.

Needs you

When it needs a decision, an approval or a login, it asks in one place, then gets on with the rest.

Tasks and routines

Hand it tasks, schedule things for later and set routines that run on their own while you're away.

Uses your browser

It works in tabs in your own Chrome profiles, signed in as you, and you can watch it live.

Hands on your Mac

It can use your Mac's apps, one at a time in the background, asking first for each app it touches.

Remembers

Facts about you, notes, your calendar and a quick daily diary, so you don't start from zero every time.

Yours

It lives with you, not with us

On a computer you own

The hut runs on your machine and keeps its data in one SQLite file there. We don't run a copy of your agent, and we don't see what it does.

Paired, not signed up

Your phone and desktop pair with a one-time code that works once, for ten minutes. Each device keeps its session in the system keychain.

A real agent inside

Each room runs a Claude Code session on your machine, with tools for your tasks, notes, calendar, messages and browser.
