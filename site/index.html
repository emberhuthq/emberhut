      Private by design

      Your machine. Your accounts. Your keys.

      • It lives on your computer

        Chats, tasks and memories stay on your machine, in a SQLite database and plain files. Its memory is Markdown you can read.

      • Your accounts, your keys

        It uses your own Claude account and your own Gmail and Slack credentials. Every token sits in a folder only your user can read.

      • No server of ours in between

        Your phone and desktop connect to your computer directly. Symploc runs no server your messages or data pass through.

      • Nothing phones home

        The software sends us no analytics, usage data or crash reports. The code is open, so you can check every line.

      What leaves your computer

      • What a task needs goes to Claude, through your own Claude Code and your own Claude account.
      • Calls to the services you connect go straight from your computer to them.
      • On Android, notifications go through Google’s push service, from your own Firebase project.
      • If you turn on the optional emberhut.com account, it lists your machines’ names and keys so your apps can find them. Never what’s on them.
      • Nothing comes to us: no analytics, no copies of your data, no way into your machine.
